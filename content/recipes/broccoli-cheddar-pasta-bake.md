**serves:** 4

## ingredients

- 2 cups gluten-free pasta (penne or rotini)
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese, divided
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons gluten-free flour or cornstarch
- 1.5 cups milk
- 0.5 tsp garlic powder
- 0.8 tsp salt
- 0.3 tsp black pepper
- 0.3 tsp mustard powder (optional but recommended)

## instructions

1. **preheat oven** to 375°F (190°C). grease a 9x13 inch baking dish.
2. **cook pasta** according to package directions, pulling it out 1-2 minutes early. drain and set aside.
3. **blanch broccoli** in the pasta water for the last 2 minutes of cooking, or steam separately until just bright green and slightly tender. drain well.
4. **make cheese sauce** — melt butter over medium heat. whisk in flour or cornstarch and cook 3 minutes. slowly pour in milk, whisking constantly. stir in garlic powder, salt, pepper, and mustard powder.
5. **add cheese** — remove from heat and stir in 1.5 cups of the shredded cheese until melted and smooth.
6. **assemble** — stir pasta and broccoli into the sauce to coat. pour into the baking dish, spread evenly, and top with the remaining 0.5 cup cheese.
7. **bake** uncovered until the cheese on top is melted, bubbly, and starting to turn golden at the edges.
8. **rest** for 5 minutes before serving — it thickens as it cools.

## notes

- undercook the pasta slightly before baking — gluten-free pasta can get mushy
- no gf flour? skip the roux and melt the cheese directly into warmed milk — a bit thinner but still good
- sharp or extra-sharp cheddar gives the most flavor
- for a crunchy topping: crushed gf crackers or breadcrumbs mixed with a little melted butter
